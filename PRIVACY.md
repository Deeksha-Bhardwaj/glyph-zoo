# Glyph Zoo — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 2026_

Glyph Zoo is a Chrome extension that replaces your new tab page with an animated ASCII scene, a clock, a daily quote and the weather.

## What Glyph Zoo stores

Your settings (your name, chosen animal and styles, city, and display options) are saved **only in your own browser** using `localStorage`. They are never sent to us or anyone else. We do not have servers, accounts or analytics.

## What Glyph Zoo sends over the internet

- **Weather (optional).** If you type a city, that **city name** is sent to [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/) to find its coordinates, and those coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo to get the current weather. No other information is sent. You can turn weather off in Tweaks, or leave the city empty.
- **Search.** When you search, the text is passed to **your own default search engine** through Chrome's search feature, exactly as if you had typed it in the address bar.

Nothing else leaves your computer.

## What Glyph Zoo does not do

- No tracking, analytics or advertising
- No collection or sale of personal data
- No access to your browsing history, tabs or other websites
